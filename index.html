// Strategic Tips
💡
Build on What You Already Have
Your Phase 1 hydrothermal data is the Research Article — don't start from scratch. Your IoT review article (~6,000 words, 27 refs) is the Book Chapter backbone. Your MANA-Pacific food system work feeds Review 1. You're assembling, not starting.
🔄
The 5-Paper Rotation Rule
With 5 manuscripts, never spend more than 2–3 consecutive days on one. Rotate strategically: when your Research Article analysis is blocked, switch to a Review introduction. Momentum feeds momentum across papers.
🌊
The Seaweed Review — Your Strongest New Piece
Pacific seaweed (Caulerpa / Nama) is directly tied to your Tupaia grant ecophysiology work. This review and your lab data reinforce each other. Prioritise building the literature base for this in Month 1 — it's a unique Pacific contribution that will attract international attention.

🛡️
When You Miss a Day
Missing one day is normal. Missing two is a pattern. Missing three needs a reset. Never try to "catch up" with double output — just continue at your normal pace. The buffer you build in good weeks covers the hard days.
🎯
100-Day Accountability Check
Every Sunday, open this app and answer 3 questions:
1. How many words did I write this week?
2. Which manuscript moved least — and why?
3. What is my one priority for next week?

That 10-minute weekly review is more valuable than any single writing session.
